\subsection{Governance and Participation Patterns}
Network analysis of Talk pages revealed uneven participation: a small number of editors accounted for a majority of comments and decision-making influence. This pattern aligns with prior research on Wikipedia’s “iron law of oligarchy”~\cite{shaw2014labor}. Nevertheless, pages with greater participation diversity showed more balanced sentiment and lower measured bias in article text, suggesting a positive link between inclusiveness and neutrality.
